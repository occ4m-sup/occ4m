OCC4M: Object-Centric 4D Memory for Spatiotemporal Reasoning in Long-Horizon Manipulation

Supplementary Materials

Simulation Experiments

T1 — Spatial Persistence

Pick the red cube and place it where the green cube was.

T1c — Viewpoint Transfer

Pick the red cube and place it where the green cube was. However, after the green cube disappears and the base moves, the model has to recover its remembered location from the new viewpoint.

T2 — Temporal Identity

Place the blue cube at the location of the marker that appeared first, and the red cube at the location of the marker that appeared second.

T3 — Event History

Pick the second object that moved and place it at the first mover's pre-motion location.

T4 — Relational Persistence

Pick the grey container cube that is currently covering the red sign.

T5 — Cross-View Association

Pick the green cube from the second table and place it where the green sign appeared on the first table. (Query color randomized per episode.)

T6 — Multi-Instance Identity

Drop every cube that was originally red into the left bin and every cube that was originally green into the right bin.

Real-World Experiments

Compositional Manipulation

Real-world Franka evaluation combining shuffled-containment recall and historical-location recall. Task: Pick up two cups in this order: (1) the cup that is now covering the red cube, (2) the cup that is now standing where the white cup used to be.

Closed-Loop Grounding Under Perturbation

Task: Pick up the cup that is still covering the red cube. In addition, the target cup is displaced during execution. OCC4M preserves its identity and updates its location online without re-querying the VLM.
